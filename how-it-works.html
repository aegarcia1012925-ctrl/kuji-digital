COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things worth knowing.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different. After learning what you need, we'll provide a scope and a project-specific quote before work starts.

How long does a website or automation take?

Timing depends on the scope, content, integrations, and feedback. We'll agree on a realistic timeline during planning.

Can you improve a website I already have?

Yes. We can discuss a redesign, focused improvements, or ways to make the existing experience clearer and easier to use.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Support needs can be discussed as part of the project plan. Any ongoing work and its cost should be agreed in advance.
